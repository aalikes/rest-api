# SOP: FBI Background Check Processing

## Overview
Standard operating procedure for processing FBI Identity History Summary (rap sheet) requests.

## Requirements
- Completed FD-258 fingerprint card
- Client consent form signed
- Privacy Act acknowledgment signed
- Payment collected

## Procedure

### 1. Intake
- Complete FBI intake form (3-step process):
  - Step 1: Personal info (name, DOB, SSN, physical descriptors)
  - Step 2: Contact info, ORI code, location preference
  - Step 3: Privacy Act consent + disclaimer
- Verify all fields are complete

### 2. Fingerprint Capture
- Follow [[SOP-Fingerprint-Capture]] procedure
- Use ORI code provided by client or default Provn ORI

### 3. FBI Submission
- Package completed FD-258 card
- Include cover letter with:
  - Client name
  - ORI number
  - Return address
  - Payment (money order or certified check)
- Submit to FBI CJIS Division:
  FBI CJIS Division
  1000 Custer Hollow Road
  Clarksburg, WV 26306

### 4. Tracking
- Update client status in system: "Fingerprints Submitted"
- Expected processing: 3–5 business days (electronic) or 12–14 weeks (mail)
- Update status as results are received

### 5. Results Delivery
- Notify client when results arrive
- Provide results via secure pickup or certified mail
- If apostille needed, proceed to [[SOP-Apostille-Workflow]]

## Privacy Act Notice
All FBI background checks are subject to the Privacy Act of 1974 (5 U.S.C. § 552a). Clients must acknowledge and consent to the collection and use of their personal information before processing.
